### Institutional Quality Standard

Mostly Alive strictly rejects claims sourced from unvetted internet blogs, commercial affiliate sites, or sensationalist social media clips. Every critical safety instruction in our guide is traceable to established statutory bodies, national emergency services, or peer-reviewed clinical guidelines.

---

### Medical & Resuscitation Authorities

- **European Resuscitation Council (ERC)** — [cprguidelines.eu](https://cprguidelines.eu/)  
  _International gold standard for Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR), Basic Life Support (BLS), and Foreign Body Airway Obstruction (FBAO)._
- **American College of Surgeons (ACS) / Stop the Bleed** — [stopthebleed.org](https://www.stopthebleed.org/)  
  _Authoritative clinical guidelines on life-threatening arterial hemorrhage control, wound packing, and commercial tourniquet protocols._
- **Wilderness Medical Society (WMS)** — [wms.org](https://wms.org/)  
  _Clinical practice guidelines for accidental hypothermia, cold water immersion, and environmental heat illness._
- **Robert Koch-Institut (RKI)** — [rki.de](https://www.rki.de/)  
  _National public health guidelines on toxic substances, epidemiological threats, and heat wave health advisories._

---

### Atmospheric & Natural Hazards

- **National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) / NWS** — [weather.gov](https://www.weather.gov/)  
  _Meteorological safety standards for lightning, convective storms, flash flooding ("Turn Around Don't Drown"), and tornadoes._
- **Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD)** — [dwd.de](https://www.dwd.de/)  
  _National meteorological safety guidance and severe weather thresholds._
- **UNESCO Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission (IOC)** — [ioc.unesco.org](https://ioc.unesco.org/)  
  _Global oceanographic consensus on coastal tsunami early warning indicators and vertical evacuation protocols._

---

### Fire & Electrical Safety

- **National Fire Protection Association (NFPA)** — [nfpa.org](https://www.nfpa.org/)  
  _International fire prevention standards, kitchen grease fire suppression, and combustible gas codes._
- **Deutscher Feuerwehrverband (DFV) & vfdb** — [feuerwehrverband.de](https://www.feuerwehrverband.de/)  
  _Standard operational guidelines on structure fire smoke behavior, low-crawl evacuation, and kitchen fire handling._
- **VDE Ausschuss für Blitzschutz und Blitzforschung (ABB)** — [vde.com](https://www.vde.com/de/blitzschutz)  
  _Engineering standards on step potential, side-flash mechanics, and lightning current dispersion._
- **Electrical Safety Foundation International (ESFI)** — [esfi.org](https://www.esfi.org/)  
  _High-voltage safety parameters and downed power line clearance procedures._

### Civil Protection & Hazardous Substances

- **Bundesamt für Bevölkerungsschutz und Katastrophenhilfe (BBK)** — [bbk.bund.de](https://www.bbk.bund.de/)  
  _German Federal Office of Civil Protection guidelines on carbon monoxide, chemical hazards, and emergency preparedness._
- **Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC - NIOSH)** — [cdc.gov](https://www.cdc.gov/)  
  _Toxicological criteria for indoor air contaminants, carbon monoxide poisoning, and environmental safety._

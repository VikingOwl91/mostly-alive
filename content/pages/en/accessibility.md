### Commitment to Accessibility

Safety and survival knowledge must be accessible to every human regardless of visual, motor, auditory, or cognitive abilities.

Mostly Alive is committed to ensuring full digital accessibility in conformance with the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA**.

### Core Accessibility Features

- **High-Contrast Dark Theme**: Designed with contrast ratios exceeding 7:1 for critical text and emergency warning blocks.
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Full keyboard operability with visible focus indicators and global shortcuts (`Cmd+K`, `/`, `Esc`).
- **Screen Reader Optimization**: Semantic HTML5 landmark structure (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<nav>`, `<article>`, `<section>`, `<footer>`) and ARIA live regions for search results.
- **Print & High-Legibility Mode**: Optimized `@media print` stylesheets that generate high-contrast, black-and-white field cards without distracting web chrome.
- **Cognitive Clarity**: Emergency actions are presented in numbered, step-by-step lists positioned at the top of pages before lengthy text.
